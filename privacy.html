Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Sologuti is a browser-based traditional board game. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the game.

Information collected by the game

The current version of Sologuti does not ask you to create an account or provide personal information. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or in-game forms. Game moves and scores are processed in your browser and are not sent to a game server.

Device storage and offline use

Sologuti uses a service worker and your browser's cache to load the app and support offline use. This stores app files on your device; it is not used by the game to identify you. Sound effects are generated locally by your browser when enabled.

Hosting

The game is hosted using GitHub Pages. When your browser requests the website, GitHub may process technical information such as IP address and request data as part of providing and securing its hosting service. Please review GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Children

Sologuti does not knowingly collect personal information from children. No account or personal details are required to play.

Changes

If the game adds features that change how information is handled, this policy will be updated before or when those features are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Sologuti project maintainer through the project's GitHub repository.
